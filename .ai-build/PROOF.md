# PROOF — What LaunchForge v0.2 demonstrates

## Engineering proof
- Next.js production build passes in CI.
- 11 executable contract tests pass in CI.
- High-impact actions are blocked by deterministic code.
- Unknown identity fails closed.
- Human handoff is a first-class tool path.
- Voice-provider-specific code is isolated from the core deployment/economic model.

## Deployment-strategy proof
- Starts from a quantified business baseline.
- Defines input/output/tool/escalation contracts before launch.
- Separates pilot readiness from production hardening.
- Makes a known edge case visible and converts it into a reusable platform requirement.
- Captures evidence provenance so synthetic results cannot masquerade as production results.

## Commercial proof
- ROI is calculated per execution.
- Every assumption can be challenged in the UI.
- The flagship model attributes €0 to speculative revenue uplift.
- The bundled synthetic sample remains economically positive despite non-perfect containment and one failed execution.

## Product proof
The reusable asset is not the Northstar demo agent. It is the control plane around deployments: blueprint, authorization boundary, evals, traces, evidence, ROI and field-learning loop.
